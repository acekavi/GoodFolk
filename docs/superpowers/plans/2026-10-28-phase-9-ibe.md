# Phase 9: Booking Site (IBE) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A public, server-rendered booking site per property, where a guest can:
- search after choosing residency;
- choose rooms and meal plans;
- book with payment at the hotel;
- manage or cancel the booking from an emailed link.

Bookings land in the PMS as ordinary reservations.

**Architecture:**
- **Public API.** core-api gains a session-less public API under `/api/public/v1/ibe/{code}`.
  - A security-definer function resolves an **enabled** site to its tenant and property. Everything after that runs tenant-scoped under RLS.
  - Search reuses `rates::load_offers` and the `tax` crate.
  - Booking reuses `reservations::create_reservation` through a new guest path.
  - Cancelling reuses `reservations::cancel_room`.
- **Mail.** Emails go through the outbox to jobs-svc, as invitations do.
- **The site.** A new SvelteKit SSR app, `web/ibe`, talks only to that API.

**Tech Stack:**
- Rust: core-api, and the `reservations`, `rates`, `tax`, `folio` and `identity` modules, plus `mail` and `jobs-svc`.
- Postgres migration 0047.
- SvelteKit 2 / Svelte 5 with SSR (`@sveltejs/adapter-node`), built with Bun.
- Cloudflare Turnstile.
- Playwright and Lighthouse CI.

**Spec:** [docs/specs/phase-9-ibe.md](../../specs/phase-9-ibe.md). Its "Settled after the builder check" section binds every task.

## Global Constraints

**Search:**
- **Residency first:** no price is shown before the guest answers "Are you a resident of Sri Lanka?".
- **Plans offered:** rate plans with segment `IBE`, a residency equal to the guest's or null, and no restriction violation.
- **Stay limits:**
  - 1 to 30 nights;
  - check-in from the property's business date up to 18 months ahead;
  - 1 to 4 rooms;
  - occupancy within each room type's maximum.
- **Prices:** in each plan's own currency, with no conversion. Totals include on-bill taxes and service charge, with a line per tax; absorbed taxes are not shown.

**Booking:**
- Payment is at the hotel: the reservation is confirmed at once, `guarantee = 'none'`, `source = 'ibe'` and `created_by` null.
- A new guest profile is created per booking and never matched by email.
- The server always re-quotes. If the price changed, the guest re-confirms. Sold out gives "That room was just booked; here is what is still available".

**Manage links:**
- The token is 32 random bytes; only its SHA-256 is stored.
- It is valid until the end of the 30th day after check-out, in the property's time zone.
- A wrong or expired token gets a uniform `410 Gone`.

**Cancelling online:**
- Allowed only while every room is tentative or confirmed and check-in is after the business date.
- The reason is "Cancelled by the guest online". The audit actor is `ibe`. The penalty comes from the snapshotted terms and is never waived.

**Public API limits:**
- **Per IP:** 60 searches and 5 bookings a minute (`429` with `Retry-After`), counted in Postgres.
- **Client IP:** `CF-Connecting-IP` only when `TRUST_CLOUDFLARE_IP=true`, otherwise the socket peer.
- **Bookings:** Cloudflare Turnstile verified on the server, plus a honeypot. `Idempotency-Key` is required and scoped to (property, key).
- **CORS:** only `IBE_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` and enabled custom hosts. No cookies.

**Caching:** `GET …/search` sends `Cache-Control: public, s-maxage=30`; `GET …/site` sends `public, s-maxage=300`.

**Settings:**
- `IbeManage` is held by the owner and the manager.
- **Validation:**
  - the accent colour must give 4.5:1 or more with white text;
  - the description is at most 500 characters;
  - the terms and the privacy notice are at most 10,000 characters each;
  - the room type description is at most 1,000 characters;
  - the `ibe_logo` upload is at most 5 MB, public;
  - the custom host is lowercase and unique across tenants.

**Performance gates:**
- `GET search` (3 nights, 2 rooms, 12 room types, 5 IBE plans): p95 < 100 ms server time.
- `POST bookings` (2 rooms): p95 < 300 ms.
- Landing and results pages: LCP < 1.5 s on Lighthouse mobile.
- At most 3 steps from the results to a booking.

**Rules carried over from earlier phases:**
- `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=3` and `-j 3`.
- Per task, only the changed crates' tests (for core-api, only the touched test binaries), plus fmt and `clippy --workspace --all-targets -D warnings`. Never `cargo test --workspace`.
- Bun only in `web/*`. No AI attribution in commits. Never push.
- `DATABASE_URL=postgres://goodfolk_owner:goodfolk_owner_dev@localhost:5432/goodfolk`. RustFS runs on :9000. Run `cargo build -p media-svc` before core-api tests.
- **Migrations with backfills under FORCE RLS** wrap the read in `no force` / `force` (0027, 0031, 0046 pattern), because the production owner is not a superuser.

## Review Focus

1. **A booking whose occupancy exceeds the room type's maximum, or whose child ages or counts are odd:** a clear field error, never a reservation. Owned by Task 3.
2. **The same Idempotency-Key reused with a different body:** `409`, never a second booking or someone else's booking returned. Owned by Task 3.
3. **A guest double-submitting or double-clicking "Book":** exactly one reservation and one email. Owned by Tasks 3 and 5.
4. **A disabled site or an unknown code, through either the path or a custom host:** `404` everywhere, including on the SSR pages, and no data leaks. Owned by Tasks 2 and 5.
5. **A guest cancelling a booking that has one room checked in, or cancelling twice:** a clear refusal, or the second cancel says it's already cancelled. Owned by Task 4.

- **`next_available`:** the first check-in within 14 days after the requested one with any offer for the same length of stay, at most 14 extra searches done as a single range read.

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Tasks 1–2.
- Produces:
  - `ibe::book(tx, tenant, property, &BookingRequest) -> Result<Booked, BookingError>`:
    - `BookingRequest { residency, booker: Booker { first_name, last_name, email, phone, country, arrival_time: Option<Time> }, rooms: Vec<BookRoom { room_type_id, rate_plan_id, meal_plan, adults, children, occupant_name: Option<String> }>, check_in, check_out, special_requests: String, expected_totals: BTreeMap<String /*currency*/, i64>, agreed: bool }`.
    - `Booked { reservation_id, confirmation_no, token: String /*returned once*/ }`.
    - `BookingError { Invalid(String), PriceChanged { totals: BTreeMap<String, i64> }, SoldOut, NotAgreed }`.
- **The route:**
  - Order of checks: limits (5 a minute per IP) → honeypot (field `website` must be empty; otherwise a fake `201` and nothing stored, **Builder's call**) → Turnstile → idempotency → `ibe::book`.
  - **Status codes:** `201` with `{confirmation_no, manage_url}`; `409 PriceChanged` with the new totals; `409 SoldOut` with the spec's copy; `422` for invalid.
- **Inside `book`:**
  - **Before anything is written:**
    - Validate the occupancy limits and that `residency` matches.
    - Re-run the search pricing for the chosen offers, and compare the totals per currency to `expected_totals`.
  - **Writing:**
    - Create the guest; occupants get guest profiles with just a name.
    - Call `create_reservation_from_site`. Map its no-availability error to `SoldOut`.
    - Insert `reservation_access_token`, with expiry at the end of check-out + 30 days in the property's time zone.
    - Write the `mail-send` outbox row. Audit `ibe.booking_created` with the reservation id and the room count, and no email or phone.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the tests**
  - `booking_creates_confirmed_ibe_reservation`: source `ibe`, guarantee `none`, `created_by` null, rooms, snapshotted cancellation terms, a new guest (a second booking with the same email gives a second guest).
  - `price_changed_needs_reconfirm`
  - `sold_out_race`: two concurrent bookings for the last room give exactly one `201` and one `SoldOut`.
  - `occupancy_over_max_is_invalid`
  - `residency_mismatch_is_invalid`
  - **core-api, the request checks:**
    - the same `Idempotency-Key` with the same body gives one booking and the same response;
    - the same key with a different body gives `409`;
    - a missing or failed Turnstile token gives `400`;
    - the 6th booking in a minute gives `429`;
    - the honeypot gives a fake `201` and no reservation.
  - **core-api, the effects:**
    - the confirmation `mail-send` row is written;
    - `jobs-svc` renders the confirmation email: subject "Your booking at <Property>: <confirmation no>", Reply-To the property's contact email, the manage link `IBE_PUBLIC_ORIGIN/<CODE>/manage?token=…`, totals per currency, the cancellation terms, and the advisory deposit if the plan has one.
